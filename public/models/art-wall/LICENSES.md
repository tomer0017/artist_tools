# 3D room assets

Sofa 02 — Kirill Sannikov, Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/a/sofa_02
Wood Floor Deck — Dimitrios Savva, Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor_deck

Both assets are CC0 1.0: https://polyhaven.com/license and https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Downloaded 27 November 2026, original 1K glTF/PBR maps. The sofa is rescaled at runtime to the selected furniture width; a material shader recolours neutral upholstery while keeping timber and surface maps. Assets are self-hosted; no external runtime requests.

Room architecture, frame/canvas geometry and procedural surface detail: created for SketchyArtLab.
Three.js and its included RoomEnvironment/OrbitControls/GLTFLoader: MIT (license in the installed three package).
