# 03 — The 50 challenges (proposal, for approval)

Fifty reusable drawing challenges. Each one can be done again and again with a
different subject ("a family member" is a different drawing every time).
People browse them, skip freely, ask for a random one at their level, or ask
for a **quick** one: a small, limited-scope drawing, never a timed task.
Finishing needs at most one optional tap: **Mark as completed**. No uploads,
no written reflection, no intermediate checkboxes.

## Fields

- **ID** — stable (`CH-001` … `CH-050`); never reused. Display order and
  grouping are separate from the ID.
- **Prompt** — the short card text (English here; Hebrew in implementation,
  gender-neutral and reviewed by a native speaker).
- **Level** — recommended difficulty: **B** beginner, **I** intermediate,
  **A** advanced. A recommendation for filtering and random picks, never a
  lock.
- **Skill** — the primary skill, with its skill area in brackets (1–10,
  below), which links it to the exercises that teach it.
- **Reference** — **Life** (draw from observation), **Own photo** (the
  learner's own photo, which never leaves the device), **Provided** (a
  licensed reference shipped with Learn), **Memory/none**.
- **Medium** — **P** pencil, **C** colour, **P/C** either.
- **Quick** — ✓ when the challenge suits a short, small drawing.
- **Tools** — optional Studio Companion shortcuts.

Counts (computed from the tables): 50 challenges; 12 beginner, 25
intermediate, 13 advanced; 15 quick. Every challenge works in pencil; 20 also
suit colour.

## Notes for the catalogue

- **Repeatable by design:** every prompt names a kind of subject, not one
  fixed picture. Provided references can grow to several per challenge, and
  a random pick may choose among them.
- **Random and quick picks** draw only from challenges the account may use,
  at or below the chosen level, and skip the ones completed most recently;
  "quick" filters on the ✓ column.
- **People in references:** CH-034 to CH-047 mostly use life or the
  learner's own photos. Any provided photo of an identifiable person needs a
  model release (see 06-architecture.md §11).
- **Suggested Free set (only a suggestion; the admin decides):** CH-002,
  CH-012 and CH-017 — a quick life drawing, a quick nature drawing, and the
  butterfly that shows Prepare Worksheets.
