# Your artwork and images — DRAFT

> **DRAFT for review by a qualified lawyer. Not legal advice; not
> published.** Facts as of 2026-11-11. This could become a section of the
> terms of service [counsel to decide].

## Your work stays yours

- **You keep all rights** in the images you use in Studio Companion and in
  everything you make with it: photos, references, studies, grids,
  comparisons, exported PNG and GIF files.
- **We claim no rights in them,** and we do not use them for anything:
  not for training, not for marketing, not for analytics.

## Your images never leave your device

- The five tools process images inside your browser. Images are not uploaded
  to our servers, and we cannot see them.
- Exports are made on your device and saved where you choose.
- Your current work is kept in your browser's storage on that device. It is
  not backed up by us. If you clear your browser data or change devices, it
  is not recoverable from us.

## Your responsibility

- Use only images you have the right to use: your own work, references you
  are allowed to use, or images whose licence permits it.
- If you share exports (for example, a comparison GIF), you are responsible
  for what you share.
- [Counsel: whether a notice-and-takedown process is needed. It is probably
  not needed while nothing is uploaded or shared through the service, but it
  would be needed if sharing features are added.]

## If this changes

If a future feature ever stores or shares images on our servers (for example,
a gallery or sharing link), it will be opt-in. We will update this page and
the privacy policy before it launches.
