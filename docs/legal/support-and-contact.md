## What we can help with

- **Signing in:**

  - for passwords, use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page.
- **Account and data:**

  - delete your account there too. Deletion is immediate and cannot be
    undone, even by support.
- **Images and work:** we cannot recover work kept in your browser, because
  it never reaches us.
- **Plans and payments:** once paid plans exist, this covers receipts,
  cancellations and refunds (see [Subscriptions and refunds]).
