## 1. Who we are

Studio Companion is operated by [legal name, registration number,
address] ("we"). For privacy questions, write to [privacy email].

## 2. The short version

- **Your images stay on your device.** The tools work on your photos and
  artwork inside your browser. We never receive, store or see them.
- **We keep what is needed to run your account:** your name, email address,
  profile picture (if you sign in with Google), settings, sign-in records and
  how much you use the features your plan limits.
- **No advertising and no third-party trackers.** We count how the service
  is used, in our own database, without your images or what you type.
- **You can download your data or delete your account** at any time from
  your account page.

## 4. Your images and artwork

The tools (Measure & Plan, Study & Simplify, Color & Mixing, Create a Grid,
Compare & Correct) process images in your browser. Images are not uploaded to
us. Exports such as PNG and GIF files are made on your device.

Your current work is saved in your browser's storage on that device. You can
remove it there: "Shared device? Sign out and remove the work stored here" on
the account page, or deleting your account (this option is on by default).

## 5. Cookies and browser storage

We use only what is needed for the service to work: sign-in cookies, a
language cookie, and your work in browser storage. See [cookies.md].

## 7. How long we keep data

- **Your account data:** until you delete your account. Password sign-ups
  that are never confirmed are deleted after 24 hours.
- **Sign-ins:** they expire after 30 days without use.
- **Usage events:** 13 months. [Proposed — confirm.]
- **Security and administration records:** 24 months. After an account is
  deleted they keep only an internal id that no longer identifies anyone.
  [Proposed — confirm.]
- **Billing records:** as long as tax law requires. [To set with the
  accountant.]
- **Backups:** after deletion, data can remain in backups for up to 14 days.

## 8. Your rights

From your account page you can:

- download your data (a JSON file);
- correct your name, language and currency;
- delete your account permanently;
- turn marketing email on or off.

For anything else, write to [privacy email]: this includes access requests,
correcting your email address, and objections. [Rights under the Israeli
Privacy Protection Law, and for EU users the GDPR where it applies — counsel
to list precisely.] You may also complain to the Privacy Protection Authority
[or your local supervisory authority].

## 9. Security

- Passwords are stored only as hashes. Email-confirmation and reset links are
  single-use or time-limited.
- Sign-in attempts are rate-limited.
- Administrators' actions are recorded.
- The service runs over HTTPS with a strict content security policy.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you as
the law requires.
