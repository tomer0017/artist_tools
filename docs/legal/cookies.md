Studio Companion uses only what it needs to work. There are no advertising,
analytics or other third-party cookies.

## Cookies

| Cookie | What it is for | How long |
|---|---|---|
| `sc.session_token` | Keeps you signed in | 30 days, renewed while you use the service; removed when you sign out |
| `sc.session_data` | A short copy of your sign-in, so pages load faster | 5 minutes |
| Temporary sign-in cookies (during "Continue with Google") | Protect the sign-in with Google from forgery | Minutes |
| `sc_locale` | Remembers your language (English or Hebrew) | 1 year |

## Storage in your browser

The tools save your current work in your browser's storage on your device:

- the project and the comparison you are working on;
- which tool introductions you have seen;
- a colour passed between tools.

This never leaves your device. You can remove it in two ways: with "Shared
device? Sign out and remove the work stored here" on the account page, or by
clearing your browser's site data.

## Your choices

All of these are needed for the service to work, so there is nothing to
switch off. [Counsel: confirm that no consent is required for strictly
necessary cookies under the applicable law.] Blocking cookies in the browser
stops you from signing in.
